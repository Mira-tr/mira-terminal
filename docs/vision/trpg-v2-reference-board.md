# RELMUA TRPG v2 Reference Board

Last reviewed: 2026-08-22

This board records reference sites for the Chikage TRPG v2 visual direction. The
goal is not to copy one site. Each reference is split into what RELMUA should
borrow, what it should reject, and which part of the experience it informs.

Primary brief:

```text
Quiet Japanese mood
Dark editorial
Interactive portfolio
Modern app UI
Mobile first
```

## Reference Set

### 1. Siteinspire

URL: https://www.siteinspire.com/

- Strength: Curated discovery by style and type. The front page exposes
  typographic, portfolio, minimal, grid, and unusual-layout categories without
  forcing a single aesthetic.
- Borrow: Use references as pattern clusters, not taste screenshots.
- Do not borrow: Do not turn RELMUA into a gallery index.
- Mobile: Category chips and compact lists support quick scanning.
- Motion: Keep the gallery behavior calm; curation is the value.
- Typography: Use category labels as navigation metadata.

### 2. Godly Websites

URL: https://godly.design/sites/

- Strength: Fast inspiration feed with explicit subcategories such as Hero, CTA,
  Footer, App Screenshots, and Icons.
- Borrow: Evaluate TRPG v2 by page part: hero, app screen, navigation, CTA,
  footer, and motion.
- Do not borrow: Do not imitate the current AI/SaaS gallery trend.
- Mobile: Reference app-screen density for Sessions and Scheduler.
- Motion: Use preview-level motion as a selection aid, not decoration.
- Typography: Let small functional labels support large editorial type.

### 3. Awwwards Art And Interactive Categories

URL: https://www.awwwards.com/websites/art/

- Strength: Useful category and tag taxonomy for animation, app style,
  fullscreen, gestures, microinteractions, parallax, scrolling, transitions,
  typography, and WebGL.
- Borrow: Use this taxonomy as a design-review checklist.
- Do not borrow: Do not add WebGL, particles, or large animation only to seem
  award-like.
- Mobile: Review whether experimental interaction survives touch input.
- Motion: Motion must map to navigation, reveal, or state.
- Typography: Treat typography as a first-class art direction category.

### 4. Dennis Snellenberg

URL: https://dennissnellenberg.com/

Research note:
https://norrly.io/inspiration/dennissnellenberg-com

- Strength: Dark elegant portfolio language with refined spacing, restrained
  accent use, magnetic interaction, and page-transition polish.
- Borrow: Near-dark canvas, limited accent, buttery transitions, and large type
  that makes the first viewport feel composed.
- Do not borrow: Do not use vivid violet as RELMUA's main identity.
- Mobile: Keep portfolio polish without reducing tap clarity.
- Motion: Buttons and page transitions can feel tactile at 200-300ms.
- Typography: Hero type should carry the atmosphere before imagery appears.

### 5. Garden Eight

URL: https://garden-eight.com/

Research note:
https://www.uicoach.io/inspirations/award-winning/garden-eight

- Strength: Japanese studio identity with dark minimalism, depth, motion, and
  strong hierarchy.
- Borrow: Use scale, restraint, and one or two material accents to produce
  confidence.
- Do not borrow: Avoid custom-cursor dependence or motion that makes app tasks
  harder.
- Mobile: Preserve the sense of authored composition even in narrow columns.
- Motion: Scroll-triggered typography can define TRPG Home only.
- Typography: Large display type may exceed the viewport when it remains
  readable by context.

### 6. Garden Eight / 601 Inc Case

URL: https://garden-eight.com/ja/cases/601/

- Strength: Case-study presentation treats role, awards, description, animation,
  and color mode as editorial data.
- Borrow: Use compact metadata rails for TRPG feature states.
- Do not borrow: Do not make every TRPG tool read like an agency case study.
- Mobile: A serial metadata stack can replace desktop side panels.
- Motion: Use sparse transition moments to separate chapters.
- Typography: Parenthetical index notation can inform TRPG section numbering.

### 7. Garden Eight / The Shift Case

URL: https://garden-eight.com/cases/the-shift/

- Strength: Separates main page, product page, and research page by role while
  preserving one design world.
- Borrow: Apply intensity rules: TRPG Home can be expressive; Scheduler and
  Admin must become calmer tools inside the same visual system.
- Do not borrow: Do not bind every page to one animation style.
- Mobile: Let page role decide density.
- Motion: Differentiate editorial reveal from product interaction.
- Typography: Each page role can change type scale without changing identity.

### 8. Kaito Note Portfolio

URL: https://kaitonote.com/works/

Award note:
https://www.cssdesignawards.com/sites/kaito-note/47392/

- Strength: Work list, filters, coordinates, and time create a personal system
  rather than a generic portfolio.
- Borrow: Numbered rows, technical metadata, and a personal spatial signature.
- Do not borrow: Do not adopt blue flame gradients as RELMUA's default look.
- Mobile: Keep filters compact and row-based.
- Motion: Filter transitions should preserve scan position.
- Typography: Pair English labels with Japanese content in a disciplined rhythm.

### 9. Kaito Note / MUUUUU Gallery Entry

URL: https://muuuuu.org/industry/art/18921.html

- Strength: The gallery tags the site for linework, page transitions, animation,
  WebGL/3D, sparse photography, geometry, and grid.
- Borrow: Use linework and geometry as visual structure for Chikage TRPG.
- Do not borrow: Do not make the prototype dependent on 3D assets.
- Mobile: Geometry must frame information instead of shrinking it.
- Motion: Page transitions can be memorable while content remains legible.
- Typography: Japanese site references validate Japanese-first type as design.

### 10. Abdul Kader Safi Portfolio Redesign

URL: https://abdulkadersafi.com/blog/i-redesigned-my-portfolio-from-tech-dashboard-to-editorial

- Strength: Clear explanation of moving from dashboard clutter to editorial
  rows, using one accent, warm near-black, warm paper, and hairline borders.
- Borrow: Rows instead of cards for Sessions, Tools, and Scenario links.
- Do not borrow: Do not copy the orange accent.
- Mobile: Disable cursor-dependent extras and keep the row structure.
- Motion: Optional hover previews belong on desktop only.
- Typography: Weight, scale, and line breaks can replace component boxes.

### 11. Roze Cook Case Study

URL: https://chintanuxui.com/case-study/roze-cook

- Strength: Brand identities are shown as a magazine-like single-page scroll
  with generous spacing and off-grid composition.
- Borrow: Treat Chikage TRPG as a personal world with several identities:
  playing, planning, recording, and remembering.
- Do not borrow: Do not split Light and Dark into equal themes for TRPG v2.
- Mobile: Preserve narrative order; avoid tabbing away the whole story.
- Motion: Idle animation can be paused or reduced.
- Typography: Editorial display type can introduce a person before function.

### 12. NEWPEACE About

URL: https://newpeace.jp/about/

- Strength: Japanese and English labels are composed as a voice system, with
  spaced English titles and strong Japanese copy.
- Borrow: Bilingual section naming and manifesto-like rhythm.
- Do not borrow: Do not adopt corporate recruiting density for TRPG Home.
- Mobile: Contents anchors can help long pages without heavy chrome.
- Motion: The page can remain mostly static if the prose has rhythm.
- Typography: Japanese headings should not feel secondary to English.

### 13. visual art / Branding by Impression

URL: https://www.visualart.jp/en/case-studies/branding-by-impression-a-top-page/

- Strength: States a useful principle: animate key moments and let surrounding
  space support calm reading.
- Borrow: Use Three.js or rich effects only later, for selected moments.
- Do not borrow: Do not add a heavy 3D scene to Scheduler or Admin.
- Mobile: Selective motion protects performance.
- Motion: Motion should guide the eye and leave a single impression.
- Typography: Motion and type should share one rhythm instead of competing.

## Reference Decision

The prototype should not look like a copied award site. It should combine:

- Garden Eight's restraint and Japanese digital craft.
- Dennis Snellenberg's dark editorial polish.
- Kaito Note's row/filter/system feeling.
- Safi's anti-card editorial row logic.
- NEWPEACE's Japanese/English voice rhythm.
- visual art's selective motion principle.
